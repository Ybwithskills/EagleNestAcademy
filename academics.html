Our Curriculum Framework

Built on the Kenya
Competency-Based
Curriculum

EagleNest Academy is a fully accredited CBC school, recognised by the Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC) and the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD). Our educators completed KICD-certified CBC training and continue with annual professional development to ensure every lesson meets the national framework.

Under CBC, learning moves away from rote memorisation toward the development of seven core competencies: Communication and Collaboration, Critical Thinking and Problem-Solving, Creativity and Imagination, Citizenship, Digital Literacy, Learning to Learn, and Self-Efficacy. Every subject, project, and assessment at EagleNest is mapped to these competencies.

What We Teach

Subjects & Learning Areas

Our CBC subject offering covers core academic areas alongside expressive arts, physical development, and digital skills.

English Language & Literature

Reading, writing, oracy, and literary appreciation. PP1 – Grade 9.

Kiswahili

Kenya's national language taught as both a subject and a medium of instruction.

Mathematics

Number, algebra, geometry, data, and financial literacy progressively from PP1 – Grade 9.

Integrated Science

Hands-on Biology, Chemistry, and Physics through inquiry experiments and project work.

Social Studies & CRE

Kenya's geography, history, citizenship, and religious and moral education.

Creative Arts & Design

Visual art, craft, design, and music integrated across all year groups.

Physical & Health Education

Athletics, team sports, gymnastics, swimming, and nutrition awareness.

Digital Literacy & Coding

ICT fundamentals, Scratch programming, and responsible digital citizenship from Grade 1.

French Language

Conversational and written French offered from Grade 4 through Grade 9.

Home Science

Nutrition, food technology, and domestic skills taught practically in Grades 7 – 9.

Agriculture & Nutrition

School garden-based learning, crop science, and sustainable food systems. Grades 4 – 9.
