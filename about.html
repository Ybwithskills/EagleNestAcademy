EagleNest Academy was founded in 1994 by [REPLACE: Founder Name(s)] with a single classroom, eight learners, and a bold conviction: that every Kenyan child deserves an education that sees their whole person — intellect, character, creativity, and spirit.

What Drives Us

Our Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

[REPLACE: e.g. To provide a transformative, CBC-aligned education that equips every learner with the knowledge, skills, values, and confidence to lead a purposeful life and contribute meaningfully to Kenyan society and the wider world.]

Our Vision

[REPLACE: e.g. To be Kenya's leading school in holistic, competency-based education — a place where every learner discovers their unique potential and every graduate leaves as a confident, compassionate, and globally competitive citizen.]

Excellence

We set and pursue the highest standards in academic achievement, character formation, and co-curricular excellence — never settling for good when great is within reach.

Integrity

Honesty, transparency, and accountability are non-negotiable — in the classroom, on the sports field, and in every interaction between learners, staff, and families.

Inclusivity

Every learner — regardless of background, ability, or learning style — is seen, valued, and given the support they need to thrive within our diverse and welcoming community.

Innovation

We embrace curiosity and creative problem-solving, preparing learners for a rapidly evolving world by fostering critical thinking, digital fluency, and entrepreneurial mindsets.

Community

Learning happens in relationship. We cultivate a warm, collaborative culture that honours family, celebrates diversity, and keeps EagleNest deeply rooted in its Kenyan identity.

Stewardship

We instil in learners a responsibility to care for our environment, our nation, and one another — shaping graduates who give back to the communities that shaped them.
