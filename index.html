The EagleNest Difference

Why Families Choose Us

Six pillars that define who we are and why our community keeps growing.

CBC-Certified Excellence

Fully accredited by KNEC and the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD), our teachers undergo continuous professional development to deliver the CBC framework at its best.

Modern Digital Learning

Smart classrooms, a fully-equipped computer lab, and a 1:1 tablet programme from Grade 4 ensure every learner is digitally fluent and ready for a technology-driven economy.

Global Perspective, Local Roots

Exchange programmes with partner schools in the UK and South Africa, a Model United Nations club, and Kiswahili & French language tracks develop citizens who are proudly Kenyan and globally aware.

Holistic Pastoral Care

Dedicated counsellors, a structured mentorship programme, and a well-being curriculum ensure every learner's mental, social, and spiritual health is supported throughout their journey with us.

World-Class Facilities

A 400-metre athletics track, Olympic-standard swimming pool, fully equipped science labs, a 600-seat performing arts centre, and a library stocked with over 15,000 titles.

Active Parent Partnership

A vibrant Parents & Teachers Association, termly progress conferences, and a real-time parent portal keep families informed, engaged, and integral to every child's educational journey.

Four Simple Steps
to EagleNest

Our admissions process is designed to be straightforward, welcoming, and transparent from the very first enquiry.

  1. 1

    Enquire & Download

    Fill in our online enquiry form or visit the school office to pick up an application pack and the current fee structure.

  2. 2

    Submit Application

    Return the completed form with certified copies of the learner's birth certificate, previous school report, and passport photos.

  3. 3

    Assessment & Interview

    Learners joining Grade 4 and above sit a short placement assessment. A brief family interview helps us understand each child's unique needs.

  4. 4

    Admission & Enrolment

    Successful applicants receive an offer letter. Secure your place by paying the admission fee and completing the enrolment pack before the term start date.
